How to use this

A short guide. Nothing here can break anything — have a look around.

What this is

A board for keeping track of where every return stands during the season. Move each client along as their return progresses, and you can see at a glance what is waiting on you and what is waiting on them.

Everything you type here stays on this computer. It is not stored online and nobody else can see it — not even ChiResearch.

The five stages

Awaiting documentsYou have taken them on, but are still waiting for their paperwork.
In preparationThe return is being worked on.
Ready for reviewFinished, waiting for your final look before it goes to the client.
Awaiting signatureSent to the client for signature. Nothing can be filed until this comes back, so this column is the one worth checking each morning.
FiledTransmitted and done.

Adding someone

  1. Click + Add client.
  2. Type their name, or a reference number if you would rather not use names.
  3. Add a short note if it helps — "waiting on 1099", "rental property".
  4. Choose the stage they are at and click Add.

Moving someone along

Use the → arrow on their card to move them to the next stage, or ← to move them back. Edit changes the name or note, and Remove takes them off the board.

Backing up — please do this weekly

Because everything lives on this computer, clearing your browsing history would erase the board. Clicking Back up saves a file to your Downloads folder that holds everything on it.

If anything is ever lost, or you move to a new computer, click Restore and choose that file.

The note at the top right of the screen tells you how long it has been since your last backup. If it turns red, it has been more than a week.

Saving and printing

  • Download spreadsheet (CSV) — opens in Excel.
  • Download report — a tidy summary you can print or send on.

Your PIN

Click Change PIN at the top to set your own four digits. The PIN keeps the board off your screen when you step away from your desk.

If you ever forget it, click Forgotten your PIN? on the sign-in screen. That puts the PIN back to the original one and does not delete a single client. Then call us and we will remind you what it is.
